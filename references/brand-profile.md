# Brand Profile

Your LinkedIn strategy in one place: who you are building yourself into, for
whom, on what topics, at a pace you can sustain, measured against targets that
matter. The `linkedin-brand-manager` skill writes this, and
`linkedin-content-planner` and `linkedin-post-writer` read it so every draft
ladders up to the strategy instead of being one-off.

Skills only read this profile when `filled: yes` below. Fill it by running
`linkedin-brand-manager` (it interviews you), or edit it directly.

> **It is a file in this repository, so git can carry it.** A filled profile goes
> wherever you push, including a public fork. Add `references/brand-profile.md` to
> your `.gitignore`, or keep the filled copy outside the repo. The bundle never
> ships your filled profile: it is on the sync exclusion list and is not copied
> into the published Codex package.
